## Historical-evidence rule

An older receipt remains authoritative for the exact bytes and interval it
names. It cannot prove V41, another platform, a longer duration, a clean host,
a signature, legal approval or independent review. A current document may link
historical evidence but must retain the original candidate and outcome.
