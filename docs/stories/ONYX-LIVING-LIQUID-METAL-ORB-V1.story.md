# Story ONYX-LIVING-LIQUID-METAL-ORB-V1: Living Liquid-Metal Humanoid Presence

## Status

**In Progress — runtime visual confirmation passed; canonical/package gates pending**

## Story

**As the** Onyx owner,  
**I want** the existing Orb to evolve into an abstract humanoid presence made of living liquid metal that breathes and reacts to every operational interaction,  
**so that** Onyx communicates presence and state without changing its approved colors or becoming visually distracting.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The exact approved palette remains unchanged: `#050607`, `#0A0D0F`, `#11161A`, `#1B2227`, `#8C949E`, `#C7C9CC`, `#0F6B68`, and `#19C7C0`; no additional visible color token is introduced.
2. The current Orb gains a liquid-alloy surface treatment using bounded layered deformation, specular flow and depth cues; it must not become a generic chrome sphere or replace the current neural identity.
3. While visible and motion is allowed, LISTENING has a calm continuous respiratory cycle; INITIALISING, THINKING, PROCESSING, SPEAKING and ERROR have recognizably different bounded motion envelopes.
4. SPEAKING responds to the smoothed audio envelope. State transitions change motion rather than color identity.
5. Reduced-motion, hidden, minimized, muted and offline modes stop recurring motion and retain a stable readable frame.
6. Rendering remains governed by the existing adaptive FPS policy, performs no network/provider work, and creates no second Python repaint loop.
7. Historical HUD evidence remains immutable. The implementation is additive through a new Orb component, shell successor and authenticated HUD acceptance successor with rollback to the prior current shell.
8. Tests prove palette preservation, bounded deformation, state mapping, reduced-motion behavior, one authoritative QML root and packaged-runtime membership.
9. The humanoid has a frontal head, neck and shoulder silhouette made from metallic contours and filaments, without literal eyes or mouth and without reproducing the referenced third-party character.
10. Its behavior follows the owner-provided motion reference: deterministic staged materialization, continuously living/breathing presence, interaction-driven internal flow, audio-responsive speech energy and bounded dissolution when transitioning offline.

## Dev Notes

- Preferred direction: **living liquid alloy** — slow anisotropic surface flow plus a restrained specular sweep, preserving the neural texture beneath it.
- Alternative retained for later evaluation: **magnetic ferrofluid** with localized state-driven lobes. It is more dramatic but risks visual noise and a darker silhouette.
- Do not use random animation, unbounded shaders, high-frequency idle loops or color changes to communicate state.
- The owner-provided Reel is a behavioral reference, not an asset source: reproduce its staged living-response qualities with original Cyryx geometry and palette.
- The audit-remediation story remains separate and must not be marked complete by this visual work.
